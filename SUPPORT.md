# Support

Use [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/bbjwz/agentstandards/discussions) for setup questions,
design discussion, and usage help. Use [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/bbjwz/agentstandards/issues)
for reproducible bugs and concrete feature requests.

Do not disclose vulnerabilities, credentials, private architecture artifacts, or provider responses
in public issues. Follow `SECURITY.md` for private vulnerability reporting.

Agentstandards is maintained on a best-effort basis. Model-provider availability, pricing, model
behavior, and third-party gateway behavior are outside the project's service guarantees.
